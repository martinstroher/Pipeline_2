CRITERION
Flag a term as an analytical framework when ANY of the following hold:
- The NLD describes a conceptual model, schema, classification scheme, or interpretive construct (e.g., "depositional model", "facies model", "stacking pattern", "stratigraphic framework", "carbonate factory" used abstractly).

- Named individuals (rdf:type entities).
- Physical entities even when they have an interpretive flavour (e.g., "carbonate factory" used CONCRETELY to mean the depositional system that produced carbonate sediment — read the NLD carefully).
- Terms that describe processes occurring in the ground (geological processes are legitimate Occurrents in BFO).
