## One authoritative fate per candidate
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- **KEEP_PRIMITIVE** — a genuine, reusable, representative kind on one primary classification axis.
- **KEEP_DEFINED** — a central/reusable cross-axis or property-defined concept worth keeping as a named class. Supply `defined_by` using only EXISTING classes/properties.
- **DEMOTE_TO_PROPERTY** — a useful distinction, but mainly a property/facet value rather than a reusable kind. Supply `demoted_as` for audit; no universal axiom will be added to the base class.
- **DROP_CLASS** — low-centrality one-off phrase, stacked qualifier, non-reusable cross-product, or unnecessary class-level detail.

A non-vacuous differentia is NOT sufficient by itself. Ask whether an expert would reuse the term as a stable kind and whether it materially serves the competency questions/domain scope.

Use frequency and CQ evidence as signals, not mechanical thresholds. A rare but central conventional kind may stay; a frequent descriptive phrase may still be demoted. Builder-minted intermediates have zero frequency by design, so judge their grouping value from the branch context.

Be conservative: when evidence is balanced, KEEP_PRIMITIVE and set `needs_review: true`. Do not drop merely because a label is compound.
