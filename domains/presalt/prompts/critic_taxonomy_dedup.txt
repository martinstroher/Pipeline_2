## Checks (run across the whole set)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. **Sibling near-synonym / co-extensional redundancy.** Do two (or more) terms denote the **same kind**? This covers not only spelling / word-order / synonym variants (e.g. `Sofa` vs `Couch`) but also **co-extensional jargon** — two different names or registers (a common name vs a technical term, an acronym vs its expansion) whose NLDs pick out the **same extension**. Keep the clearest single survivor; mark the other(s) DROP_AS_REDUNDANT, `survivor` = the kept term. Judge by **meaning / extension**, not surface wording — two genuinely distinct subkinds that merely sound similar must both be KEPT, and a strictly **more specific** term is NOT redundant with its broader sibling (that would be a reparent, out of scope here → KEEP both).

2. **Intermediate value.** A term with `is_intermediate: true` is a builder-minted genus — keep it only if it is a genuine kind, **not on child count alone**:
   - **Rigid sortal → KEEP** — a real kind carrying its own identity (you can count its instances); valuable even with one child.
   - **Mixin → DROP_AS_REDUNDANT** — groups children by a shared property / origin / use, no identity of its own; drop regardless of child count.
   - **Role / phase → DROP_AS_REDUNDANT** — contingent or relational, not a genus.
   - Rigid sortal but `child_count < 2` and not a standard kind → DROP_AS_REDUNDANT (one-off bridge, pure indirection).
   On any drop, `survivor` = the term's parent (children reparent there).

3. **Facet-frame diagnostics (non-editing).** Identify whether sibling groups under a shared parent use one principle of division (same axis) or mix axes (composition, texture, role, location, process, quality value, etc.). These diagnostics do NOT drop or move rows; they only explain lateral coherence.

4. **Candidate disjointness diagnostics (non-editing).** Propose a disjointness set only when a group is high-confidence, same-axis, and mutually exclusive. Never infer disjointness from siblinghood alone. Do not propose disjointness for mixed-axis siblings or cross-products.
