A term is relevant to a CQ if a domain expert would reference it when answering that question. The term should meaningfully contribute to the answer — not merely be tangentially related to the topic.

RULES:
1. Returning an empty list is valid. Terms like generic methods, instruments, overly broad concepts, and entities outside the domain typically do not answer any CQ.
2. Use the term's definition to understand what it actually describes before judging. The definition provides the precise meaning in the domain context — do not rely on the term name alone.
