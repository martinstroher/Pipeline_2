Measurement / property terms should be modelled as OWL DatatypeProperty restrictions on classes — they do NOT earn standalone class status in a domain ontology.

Be decisive. If a term's NLD describes a property OF something rather than a thing in itself, flag it for removal.

[PROMPT_TEMPLATE]

BATCH (JSON array of {{term, nld, category}}):
{batch_json}

CRITERION
Flag a term as a measurement / property when ANY of the following hold:
- The NLD describes a measurable quantity, magnitude, fraction, or score (temperature, thickness, depth, porosity %, permeability, density, salinity, grain size, sorting coefficient, dip angle, acoustic impedance, displacement, recovery factor, …).
- The NLD reads as "the [degree/amount/extent] of X" rather than "an X that …".
- The term is named after a measurement instrument's readout (gamma ray value, resistivity log reading, …) rather than the thing being measured.

- Named individuals (the input row will indicate this via category or NLD).
- Rock-physics PROPERTIES that the field treats as named geological qualities under GeoCore (e.g., "porosity" as a Geological Quality is acceptable when the surrounding ontology models it as such — only flag if the term is clearly intended as a class of objects).
- Terms that are quantitative IN PASSING but whose NLDs describe an entity (e.g., "thin section" is a sample, not a thickness measurement).

OUTPUT — a JSON array with one object per input term, in the same order:
